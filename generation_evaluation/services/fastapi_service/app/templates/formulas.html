Which matter most in production

  • {{ priority('critical') }}
    Faithfulness, Answer Relevancy, Answer Correctness
    Stop hallucinations, off-topic and wrong answers. They decide pass/fail.
  • {{ priority('high') }}
    Answer Completeness, Semantic Similarity
    Catch missing details and check meaning. They also decide pass/fail.
  • {{ priority('low') }}
    Exact Match, BLEU, ROUGE
    Word-overlap scores: useful for comparing text, too strict for natural answers. Shown, but never fail an answer on their own.
