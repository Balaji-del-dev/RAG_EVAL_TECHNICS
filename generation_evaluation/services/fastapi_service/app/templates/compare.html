How to read it

  • Word-overlap metrics (Exact Match, BLEU, ROUGE) punish the paraphrase even though it is right. They compare words, not facts.
  • Semantic Similarity rewards the paraphrase, but it also scores the hallucinated answer high: “20 casual and 15 sick” reads almost the same as “12 and 12”. Embeddings do not check numbers.
  • Faithfulness and Correctness (LLM judge) are the metrics that catch the hallucinated answer. That is why they are critical in production.
  • Completeness catches the incomplete answer; Relevancy catches the irrelevant one.
