What gets measured

Precision@K
Relevant in top K / K
Recall@K
Relevant in top K / all relevant
F1
Harmonic mean of precision and recall
Hit Rate@K
1 if any relevant chunk is in the top K
MRR
1 / rank of the first relevant chunk
NDCG@K
DCG / ideal DCG, using grades 0-3

Precision, Recall, F1 and Hit Rate count grade 1 or higher as relevant. NDCG uses the grades themselves (gain 2grade − 1 by default).
