File formats

JSON, with an optional chunk corpus for the built-in retrievers:

{
  "name": "HR Policy Evaluation",
  "version": "1.0",
  "chunks": [
    {"chunk_id": "C101", "title": "Maternity eligibility",
     "text": "..."}
  ],
  "queries": [
    {"query_id": "Q1",
     "text": "What is the maternity leave policy?",
     "ground_truth": [
       {"chunk_id": "C101", "grade": 3},
       {"chunk_id": "C102", "grade": 2}]}
  ]
}

CSV, one row per ground-truth label:

query_id,query,chunk_id,grade
Q1,What is the maternity leave policy?,C101,3
Q1,What is the maternity leave policy?,C102,2

Grades: 3 highly relevant, 2 relevant, 1 somewhat relevant, 0 not relevant. Every query needs at least one chunk with grade 1 or higher.
